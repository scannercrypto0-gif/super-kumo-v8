## Owner amendment — Outcome-to-Precursor Discovery (required)
Discoverer observes **both significant losses and gains**, not just market leadership or pre-event signals. For each detected DOWN/UP event, freeze the instrument, venue, start/end, event definition, severity, membership and as-of observation; **request from Core** source-backed pre-event market indicators, liquidity, sector/BTC/PAXG context, Scanner MONITOR_ONLY features and news/macro available **before event onset**, with explicit lookback windows and known-at/published/ingested clocks. Never use revised or subsequently published facts as if available beforehand. Compare with historical analogous UP/DOWN events and with **matched no-move / contrary-move controls**; record failed analogues, missing observations, sample selection and denominator. Extract falsifiable candidate precursors, then **reverse-test prospectively** whether those precursors predict anything beyond baseline and costs. Event-first discovery is hypothesis generation, **not** proof of causality or deployable predictive power. Version the event→Core query→precursor evidence→hypothesis→Factory test→feedback chain. The same discovery loop also runs **forward continuously** to identify patterns before any large move. The research loop is both retrospective event-to-precursor and forward precursor-to-outcome.
