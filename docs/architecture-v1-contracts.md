# Architecture v1 contract specification

[Architecture](architecture-v1.md) · [Acceptance](architecture-v1-acceptance.md) · Provisional wire and logical state specification. Native schemas remain authoritative at their current interfaces. An adapter maps a native immutable record by exact version and digest, including its evidence grade. Add a field or adapter only for a demonstrated gap.

## Shared types and delivery

Schema grammar: fields are required unless suffixed with `?` (omittable). `T|null` requires a key and allows explicit null. Arrays preserve canonical order; keys marked unique reject repeats. `id` is a nonempty case-sensitive opaque identifier; `sha` is lowercase 64-character SHA-256 hex; `ms` is a nonnegative UTC Unix epoch integer in milliseconds; `uint` is a nonnegative integer; `dec` is a finite exact base-10 string without exponent, with units declared by the enclosing field; `bool` is JSON boolean; `text` is nonempty UTF-8; literal alternatives form a closed enum. Probabilities are decimals in [0,1]. All windows are half-open. IDs are scoped by contract major and owner namespace. `Ref={schema:id,id:id,version:id,digest:sha}` resolves immutable bytes. `Market={venue:id,market_type:SPOT|DERIVATIVE,base:id,quote:id,symbol:id,calendar:UTC}`. `Strategy={id:id,version:id,definition:Ref}`. Monetary values declare currency; rates declare units. Field blocks specify the JSON object members, including nested inline shapes. Each named `.v1` implies major 1.

**Envelope.v1** adds `schema:id, artifact_id:id, artifact_version:id, producer:id, correlation_id:id, causation:Ref|null, created_at:ms, as_of:ms, valid_until:ms|null, policy:Ref, evidence:TEST_ONLY|RETROSPECTIVE_NON_PIT|PROSPECTIVE_UNATTESTED|PIT_ATTESTED, quality:OK|PARTIAL|UNKNOWN|INVALID, reason:id|null, parents:Ref[], payload_digest:sha, payload:object`. Canonical UTF-8 JSON for new envelope bytes uses lexicographic keys, compact separators, no floats or duplicate keys, and preserves array order. SHA-256 of the canonical payload binds payload; reference digest binds the complete artifact. Existing seals retain their native canonicalization and an adapter records both digests. Correlation is inherited across a run, causation names the immediate parent, and a new version cites the prior ref. Unknown mandatory facts require reason and fail eligibility. Evidence cannot be upgraded by an adapter, cache, replay, match or simulation. Only independently qualified custody and chronology allow PIT_ATTESTED. Science partitions and observed/simulated fills remain distinct axes.

**SourceClock** `{event_at:ms|null,published_at:ms|null,observed_at:ms|null,ingested_at:ms|null,known_at:ms|null,witness:Ref|null}`. Event, publication, receipt and commit clocks remain distinct. Known-at is the earliest independently supportable availability to the specified consumer and requires witness; source-publisher first availability remains a distinct field when supported. Receipt cannot precede a comparable event and commit cannot precede receipt. Future scheduled event time may exceed as-of when the schedule announcement was already known. Causal input requires its relevant revision known by cutoff and locally committed by decision freeze; replay cannot substitute later learned material. Retrospective computations may use structural close with NON_PIT grade.

**DeliveryReceipt.v1** · Secretary routes; immediate consumer produces durable acceptance; Integration reconciles. Input `{artifact:Ref,consumer:id,route:id,obligation_id:id,available_at:ms,expires_at:ms,retry_policy:Ref}`. Output `{obligation_id:id,artifact:Ref,consumer:id,state:WAIT|ACK|RETRY|REJECTED|EXPIRED|DEAD_LETTER|UNKNOWN,attempt:uint,at:ms,next_attempt_at:ms|null,reason:id|null,commit_ref:Ref|null}`. Uniqueness is producer artifact identity/version + route + recipient. Sender records obligation with committed artifact (existing journal transaction/outbox or deterministic recovery scan). Recipient validates version/hash/expiry and atomically dedupes with effect and durable receipt before ACK. Sender ACK projection follows verified readback. Duplicate same digest returns original receipt; conflicting digest quarantines. Retry only transient failure, using frozen max attempts/deadline/backoff/jitter, bounded by expiry. Permanent schema/policy failure is REJECTED. Ambiguous commit is UNKNOWN until readback; no second effect. Expired delivery cannot revive. On exhausted retries retain dead-letter evidence and reconcile by obligation/ref from durable cursor, with an explicit operator replay record for a repaired eligible obligation. No new bus or parallel truth store. ACK proves durable recipient custody only. Crash before/after commit, lost ACK, duplicate, reorder and late delivery must preserve exactly one logical acceptance.

Common receiver rules apply to every contract below. A missing required field, incompatible major, wrong subject, broken ref or unknown state rejects or quarantines. Optional additive changes require declared consumer compatibility and omission-safe behavior; never reinterpret old fields/enums. New semantics require a major and version-pinned dual read during migration; a prior ACK applies only to its exact ref. Freeze numeric limits in referenced policy before runs; absent policy fails closed. Delivery expiry is separate from immutable retention. Rights govern retention; where lawful preserve minimal tombstone, digest and reason after removal. A tombstone cannot support revalidation of missing evidence.

## Lifecycle and permissions

**StrategyLifecycle.v1** · Factory → Lab, Risk, Paper and Reporting. Input `{strategy:Strategy,prior:Ref|null,trigger:Ref,actor:id,expected_seq:uint}`. Output `{strategy:Strategy,seq:uint,state:EXPERIMENTAL|RESEARCH_ACCEPTED|RELEASE_ELIGIBLE|PAPER_ELIGIBLE|PAPER_ACTIVE|PAPER_PAUSED|RETIRED|STOP|UNKNOWN,protocol:Ref,lab_verdict:Ref|null,paper_eligibility:Ref|null,reason:id|null}`. Append with compare-and-set; new strategy version starts EXPERIMENTAL. Lab outcome is immutable and independent. Permission grants do not mutate scientific or paper state.

| Transition | Authority and guards |
| --- | --- |
| EXPERIMENTAL → RESEARCH_ACCEPTED | Factory applies Lab's frozen scientific PASS with adequate grade/coverage. Engineering PASS, synthetic and retrospective-only proofs cannot satisfy prospective claims. |
| RESEARCH_ACCEPTED → RELEASE_ELIGIBLE → PAPER_ELIGIBLE | Factory checks pinned release/adapters, approved phase, reproducibility, then current risk and simulation acceptance. |
| PAPER_ELIGIBLE → PAPER_ACTIVE | Owning paper trader starts automatically under enabled phase and valid eligibility, without per-strategy or per-trade owner approval. Factory records its start receipt. |
| PAPER_ACTIVE → PAPER_PAUSED/STOP/UNKNOWN | Trader or risk veto feeds Factory. Suspend new entries on evidence loss, stale data, operator stop, health/lease fault or limit breach. Manage existing simulated positions under frozen recovery policy. |
| PAPER_PAUSED/STOP/UNKNOWN → PAPER_ELIGIBLE | Reconcile positions, recover exact input/policy evidence, clear stop under owning policy and recheck eligibility. Never jump directly to ACTIVE. |
| Any nonretired → RETIRED | Factory records withdrawal or policy failure, with safe position wind-down and lineage. Terminal for that version. |

**PaperTradingEligibility.v1** · Risk → trader/Factory. Input `{strategy:Strategy,trader:id,release:Ref,evaluation:Ref,simulation_policy:Ref,risk_policy:Ref,phase:Ref,health:Ref}`. Output `{strategy:Strategy,trader:id,state:ELIGIBLE|DENIED|UNKNOWN|EXPIRED,scope:Market[],max_notional:dec,currency:id,max_open:uint,checked_at:ms,expires_at:ms,reason:id|null}`. Require current release, version, freshness, risk/stop checks on every decision. No grant conversion.

**PermissionGrant.v1** · Owner → permission verifier. Input owner-authenticated command bytes. Output `{grant_id:id,owner_id:id,capability:TELEGRAM_SIGNAL_AUTHORIZED|LIVE_AUTHORIZED,strategy:Strategy,trader:id,scope:Market[],environment:id,destination:id,policy:Ref,issued_at:ms,valid_from:ms,expires_at:ms,nonce:id,proof:Ref}`. One capability per grant. Authenticated proof binds exact payload; verification checks owner identity, replay nonce, current revocation/stop, clock, strategy/trader/environment/market/destination and policy at every external effect. Missing, expired, UNKNOWN or wrong scope denies. Persist auditable decision and permission epoch. A stale cached grant cannot authorize a send/order.

**PermissionRevocation.v1** · Owner → verifier and consumers. Input signed revocation of exact grant or enumerated scope. Output `{revocation_id:id,owner_id:id,target_grants:id[],effective_at:ms,epoch:uint,reason:id,proof:Ref}`. Append before downstream ACK; invalidate leases and queued effects, fence stale workers, reconcile already in-flight effects and cancel/contain under separately authorized policy. Revoked IDs never reactivate. Emergency Operations stop is independent veto, not grant/revoke authority. Verification and effect admission share a serialized fence; effects already transmitted are reconciled, never retroactively erased.

**TelegramSignal.v1** · Reporting publisher → explicitly granted recipient. Input `{grant:Ref,strategy:Strategy,trader:id,decision:Ref,signal_id:id,prior_signal:Ref|null,kind:ENTRY|AMEND|CANCEL|EXIT,market:Market,side:BUY|SELL,price:dec|null,uncertainty:text,decided_at:ms,received_at:ms,fresh_until:ms,execution_status:SIMULATED|NO_EXECUTION|INDEPENDENTLY_ATTESTED}`. Output `{signal_id:id,destination:id,state:SUPPRESSED|SENT|UNKNOWN|EXPIRED,provider_receipt:Ref|null,at:ms,reason:id|null}`. Opt-in actionable channel is distinct from read-only reports. Check grant and freshness at dequeue and send, maintain unique destination/signal/version, reconcile ambiguity without blind resend. AMEND/CANCEL/EXIT reference prior signal. Manual owner activity remains outside ATLAS unless independently evidenced and separately classified; delivery is never a venue fill.

**LiveTradingAuthorization.v1** · Permission verifier → future live execution adapter. Input `{grant:Ref,strategy:Strategy,trader:id,account_scope:id,market:Market,intent:Ref,requested_notional:dec,currency:id,venue_risk:Ref,health:Ref}`. Output `{authorization_id:id,grant:Ref,intent:Ref,state:ALLOWED|DENIED|UNKNOWN|REVOKED|EXPIRED,checked_at:ms,expires_at:ms,epoch:uint,reason:id|null}`. Require separate LIVE grant and validated account/release/limits/stop status. Bind single-use authorization to intent and stable client order ID. Reconcile ambiguous venue responses before retry; venue execution requires independent venue evidence. Telegram grant or paper eligibility cannot authorize this contract.

| Action | Required authority |
| --- | --- |
| Discover, test, classify, release to paper, simulate | Frozen admissible-source/science/release/risk and phase policy, automatic within scope |
| Release final holdout or activate paid provider | Separate existing owner governance and custody |
| Publish nonactionable report | Reporting policy and source disclosure rights |
| Publish actionable real-time Telegram | Personal owner TELEGRAM grant and use-time checks |
| Trade with real funds | Personal owner LIVE grant plus separate venue/risk activation and use-time checks |
| Stop | Owning risk/Operations veto; Owner may also revoke. AI/ML, Secretary and ACK cannot confer authority. |

Attempts, decisions/opportunities and trials are independent populations with explicit IDs, counts and frozen membership. A trial rerun does not create a new opportunity; an omitted/denied opportunity remains in the census. Publish numerator, eligible population, exclusion reasons, unknown count, and coverage by partition. Compare same-opportunity observations without dividing only by trades filled. Validate missing/duplicate/swapped/tampered lineage by digest, IDs, original census membership and cross-reader agreement. Use existing Factory registry and results ledger rather than creating separate feedback or model truth stores.

Acknowledgement is the common output for every typed handoff, including reference-only input. Source READY means a committed admitted query; Scanner state belongs to its census; delivery terminal state belongs to the obligation; research verdict belongs to Lab; promotion belongs to Factory's lifecycle projection; position/fill assumptions belong to the trader. No downstream consumer rewrites the preceding owner's record.
