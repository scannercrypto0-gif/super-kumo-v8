# ATLAS Quant Lab Architecture & Engineering Contract v1.0 — DRAFT
Project ID: `AQL-CRYPTO-RESEARCH-001`. Former project name: SUPER KUMO V8. Historical ATLAS-001 updated identity only; the provisional TIME/DATA addendum below now explicitly refines temporal eligibility.
Goal: Binance Spot USDT research platform, momentum first, then pre-run and accumulation; independent paper traders; news/event intelligence; controlled shadow experiments.
Architecture: Python modular monolith, shared market-data adapter/cache, point-in-time feature engine, observation and outcome tracker, versioned trader plug-ins, deterministic paper fill engine, event adapters, research lab, scheduler, Supabase operational storage, durable local offline spool. Bulk historical datasets may live in object storage.
Trader output: WATCH / ARMED / TRIGGERED / CLOSED, plus EXPIRED / CANCELLED. Conditional entry, invalidation, stops, targets and expiry. Independent same-symbol trades; flag correlated outcomes.
Paper accounting: no portfolio or capital allocation initially; record realistic fill assumptions, fees, slippage, R, MFE/MAE, no-fill and ambiguous-bar cases. No live execution.
Research: observe executed, rejected, expired and non-selected opportunities; forward 4/24/72h returns; historical replay where point-in-time data exists. No retrospective leakage. Register every experiment; freeze parent and shadow versions; paired comparisons and untouched forward test; owner approves promotion.
Events: unified adapters for macro/Fed, token unlocks, news, social, search; record event_time, published_at, observed_at, provenance and licensing. Source access/cost may limit initial coverage.
Data (provisional G0-TIME-01/02 refinement): closed bars eligible at decision cutoff; intra-candle/live observations eligible only with verifiable exact decision-time availability evidence, fallback to qualifying completed candles only when explicitly permitted by the trader's predeclared policy and availability/freshness checks; otherwise block the affected decision and record why. UTC event/receipt/availability/decision times, versioned features and schemas, immutable decisions, separate future labels. Errors not reclassified as trading rejects.
Operations: adaptive API-weight-aware scans, cache, retry/backoff, idempotency, offline spool, restore tests, telemetry, secrets in environment, migration control.
Change control: all interface/schema changes require ADR; every work package needs scope, non-goals, measurable tests, evidence, rollback, reviewer verdict and owner signoff.
Known unknowns: API budgets, Supabase retention/cost, provider rights, PC restart reliability, exact paper fill model. These are work packages, not assumptions.

## Provisional paper-trading invariants — G0-PAPER-01–08

**Status: PROVISIONALLY OWNER-APPROVED — NOT FINAL G0 APPROVAL.**
The detailed owner-selected rules, rationale, extensions and unresolved parameters are canonical in [03_DECISIONS.md](03_DECISIONS.md#g0-paper-trading-decisions--owner-selected-provisional-rules). This addendum refines the draft only; no functionality is implemented or final G0 approval granted.

- G0-PAPER-01: conditional fills only; immediate entry uses the first eligible observable price after signal availability. No-fill, expiry and rejection remain distinguishable; no look-ahead.
- G0-PAPER-02: legitimately available finer data may resolve ordering. Otherwise factual AMBIGUOUS persists; separate worst-stop-first/best-target-first research scenarios propagate to aggregate performance. Best case is not verified realized performance; ordering and cost uncertainty stay separate.
- G0-PAPER-03/04: record gross, baseline net and stress-cost results with versioned assumptions, fee provenance/effective dates and liquidity evidence. Reference notional is provisionally 1,000 USDT; 100/10,000 USDT are proposed sensitivity examples, not portfolio sizing or live risk policy. Missing data cannot imply perfect execution; no fixed Binance fee is adopted.
- G0-PAPER-05/06: traders and Shadows remain independent; deduplicate materially identical active opportunities within each trader under explicit identity rules, report overlap/correlation, and allow Telegram grouping without record merging. Freeze strategy-specific validity/invalidation policies; entry expiry differs from post-fill holding duration. No universal Momentum expiry is fixed.
- G0-PAPER-07: common exit evaluator supports predeclared strategy-specific exit rules. Snapshot rules at trade creation, timestamp permitted adjustments, separate exit reason/price/cost, and retain chronological ambiguity. Future partial exits remain deferred. Post-exit observation and fixed/raised-stop comparisons belong to isolated reproducible Lab/Shadow paths, never rewriting original closed trades.
- G0-PAPER-08: durable recovery retains pending/open state, versions and progress; replay missing eligible market events chronologically and idempotently. Separate market/ingestion/recovery times, preserve corrections with audit history, never exit solely at stale prices, and never fabricate real-time decisions. Unrecoverable gaps remain unresolved and outside confirmed performance unless resolved or transparently scenario-analyzed.

Exact execution parameters, strategy TP/SL values, stop timing, partial quantities, post-exit horizons and promotion thresholds remain undecided. Numerical capital brainstorming in the register is explicitly nonbinding. Temporal/research/operations questions beyond this subset and final independent review remain open. No live trading or portfolio implementation is authorized.

## Provisional TIME and DATA invariants

**PROVISIONALLY OWNER-APPROVED — NOT FINAL G0 APPROVAL.**
Canonical details: [G0 TIME and DATA decisions](03_DECISIONS.md#g0-time-and-data-provisional-decisions) and [mandatory clarifications A–F](03_DECISIONS.md#time-and-data-mandatory-owner-clarifications). No implementation is claimed.

### Time eligibility and immutable context — TIME-01–04,07–09
Event time alone never establishes knowledge: receipt and effective availability must precede decision use; monitor clock skew/quality. A qualifying completed candle is a fallback only when explicitly allowed by the trader's predeclared policy and availability/freshness checks; otherwise block the affected decision and log why. Historical assumptions are explicitly modeled replay, distinct from observed-live or verified historical availability. News preserves original publication and time added to ATLAS plus verified observation/content versions; later edits/imports cannot fabricate old ingestion. Scheduled announcements differ from unexpected news. Corrected market datasets create new shared reproducible versions and affected-evidence review, distinct from simulator/code corrections.

Every opportunity (executed/rejected/expired/no-fill) references rich feasible context: BTC dominance/trends, BTC/ETH and ETH/BTC, altseason/breadth, liquidity/volatility and asset-relative strength plus justified indicators. Freeze original decision snapshot; open-position changes append timestamped events. Source/version/freshness/missingness and shared references distinguish known-at-decision, assumption-based reconstruction and retrospective explanation. Freshness is indicator-specific: stale/missing essential inputs block affected NEW entries, optional gaps do not halt unrelated traders. No hindsight regime label authorizes an entry; existing positions follow frozen exits, with alternatives isolated as Research/Shadow versions.

### Specialist research reporting — TIME-05–07
Freeze strategy/rule/code/configuration/dataset references. Separate historical development, historical out-of-sample and prospective forward sections side by side, never a combined success score or backdated Shadow evidence. Evaluate multiple regimes/independent target occurrences where data permits, chronological walk-forward and untouched final holdout with leakage controls. Every candidate/forward trader reports target/off-target counts, net costs, drawdown/risk, uncertainty and sample adequacy; insufficient evidence is not profitability. A specialist is not rejected solely for off-target weakness; regime-detector decision-time reliability is evaluated independently. Investigate historical/forward divergence (regime shifts/errors, costs, quality, overfit). Forward entries require predeclared regime eligibility using available information; nomination is not automatic promotion.

### Shared acquisition and library preparation — DATA-01–03
Central collector plans/deduplicates acquisition, shares freshness-aware cache, computes derivable indicators locally and coordinates actual endpoint/provider weights/backoff without invented numeric limits. Define provider or local method, provenance, freshness, API budget and availability limits per external indicator.
A separately scheduled pre-operational initial backfill aims for broad trustworthy economically feasible coverage including context/news/events where sources/rights allow; never make archive completion a synchronous core-run dependency. Coverage inventory declares scope, assets/markets/timeframes/dates, indicators/archives, gaps/unsupported sources/quality, progress/validation/versions; claim completeness only when verified. Incremental ingestion/repair update that SAME library after launch. Compressed partitioned archive plus Supabase/PostgreSQL metadata, shared immutable checksummed objects and selective retrieval are provisional; Parquet remains a candidate pending latency/footprint/transfer/cost measurement.

### Critical-path isolation and cost gates — DATA-02–04
Core Live Run (live monitoring, open paper positions and eligible decisions) has highest priority; context, archive-maintenance and research/report jobs have independent schedules/queues and bounded concurrency within the modular monolith. No archive downloads, historical repairs, news enrichment, backtests or heavy reports may be synchronous prerequisites of core completion. Shared request dedup/API budgets and CPU/memory/I/O/DB/network reservations or equivalent controls prevent starvation; background work yields first. Bound overlapping core cycles/backlog; monitor duration, deadline misses, freshness, queue depth and budgets. Essential gaps mean honest degraded/unknown status, never fictional fills; historical repair must not fabricate live decisions. Incremental repairs are resumable/idempotent and validate missing ranges before publication.
Minimize avoidable cost and reuse data; never guarantee zero cost. Monitor provider/storage/transfer/compute budgets and require owner approval BEFORE any new paid source, storage upgrade, compute resource or chargeable service. Research growth cannot silently incur expenses. Thirty seconds is illustrative only; measured load benchmarks establish objectives later.

This remains paper-only G0, compatible with pending [PAPER decisions in PR #7](https://github.com/scannercrypto0-gif/super-kumo-v8/pull/7); no merge, code, infrastructure, strategy promotion or final architecture approval is authorized.

## Provisional live-data operations — G0-DATA-05–08
**PROVISIONALLY OWNER-APPROVED — NOT FINAL G0 APPROVAL.**
[Canonical decisions](03_DECISIONS.md#g0-live-data-operations-decisions--data-0508); pending TIME/DATA foundations in PR #8 and PAPER rules in PR #7 remain provisional, not merged. No application implementation.

- DATA-05: centralized Binance Spot WebSocket observations where suitable, REST initialization/reconciliation/validation/bounded gap repair; shared cache/request dedup and one budget authority for REST weights AND connection/subscription limits. Controlled reconnect/backoff prevents storms. Event/receipt/availability/processing/decision/source evidence distinguishes live-observed from later REST recovery. No retroactive live signals or fake fresh prices. Existing closed-bar feature defaults do not preclude a distinct exact-availability-verified live observation under proposed TIME-01/02; later finalized OHLCV is never earlier live evidence.
- DATA-06: deadline-aware bounded non-accumulating core runs, with measured duration/lag/deadline misses/queue depth/skipped/coalesced records. Safe current-scanning snapshot coalescing must NEVER discard event sequences needed for paper-position monitoring, fills/ambiguity, chronological replay or audit. Skipped scans are not fabricated live opportunities. Retried event processing remains deterministic/idempotent; no fixed 30-second SLA.
- DATA-07: logically independent higher-priority Position Monitor evaluates frozen TP/SL/time/trailing/early-exit rules without waiting for full-universe scanning. Lower-priority Opportunity Scanner uses bounded/adaptive batches and freshness-qualified regime inputs; under load scanning yields first, with reason logs. Both reuse central ingestion/context/cache/provider budgets, preserve independent trader/rule/decision identity, and protect CPU/memory/I/O/DB/network from archive/news/research/report work. Modular monolith remains; separate deployed services are not required.
- DATA-08: source/symbol/stream/data-type feed health is conceptually HEALTHY/DEGRADED/UNAVAILABLE with integrity/continuity/provider liveness/sequence/freshness/reconciliation evidence and indicator-specific TTL. No recent trades alone does not prove disconnection. Only affected decisions block; independent healthy workloads may continue. Outage positions remain OPEN with gaps/uncertainty; never invent price/fill/exit or retroactive decisions. Bounded budgeted reconnect/REST reconciliation chronologically/idempotently recovers available facts, preserving unresolved ambiguity and residual gaps, without guaranteed recovery/uptime.

Initial Research Library preparation and maintenance stay outside the critical core path; shared budget authority applies to every loop. Future read-only monitoring should display feed state, lag and missingness without implying trading authority; dashboard implementation remains deferred. Exact limits/streams/endpoints/cadences/TTL/deadlines await documentation/measurement, not this PR.

## Reconciled point-in-time eligibility — INT-04
Closed completed-candle values require completion AND demonstrated availability to the trader by decision time. Live trades/prices and provisional intrabar indicators are separate observation classes; record candle completion state, source, calculation/data version, market event time, system receipt time, effective availability time and decision time. Never use final candle high/low/close before completion and availability, or backdate later-recovered/revised values. Fallback to a qualifying completed candle is permitted ONLY when the trader's predeclared policy explicitly allows it; otherwise block the affected decision and record the reason. All essential inputs must also meet trader-specific freshness policy (TIME-09).
